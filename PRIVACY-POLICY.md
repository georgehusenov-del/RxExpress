# RxEx Driver Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 17, 2026

RxEx Driver is used by delivery drivers to manage prescription deliveries, verify assigned stops, scan packages, capture proof of delivery, and share live delivery status with RxExpresss backend systems.

## Information We Collect

### Account and profile information
- Name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Driver profile details such as vehicle type, vehicle number, license number, and driver status

### Location information
- Foreground location while the app is in active use
- Delivery location coordinates used to confirm active route status and proof of delivery

The app does not request background location permission.

### Camera and delivery evidence
- QR code scans used to verify packages
- Delivery photos captured for proof of delivery
- Recipient signatures when required for a delivery
- Delivery notes and failed-attempt reasons entered by the driver

### Technical and session data
- Secure authentication token stored locally on the device using platform secure storage
- Network request logs and limited diagnostic information needed to operate the service

## How We Use Information

We use collected information to:
- Authenticate drivers and restore secure sessions
- Show assigned deliveries and delivery history
- Verify packages during scanning
- Send live delivery status and foreground route updates
- Capture and submit proof of delivery
- Record copay collection and failed delivery attempts
- Maintain operational, security, and audit records for prescription delivery workflows

## Data Sharing

Information collected through RxEx Driver is transmitted to RxExpresss backend services and may be accessed by authorized operational staff, dispatch staff, pharmacy staff, administrators, and service providers supporting the delivery workflow.

We do not sell personal information collected through the app.

## Data Retention

Delivery records, proof-of-delivery materials, and related operational data may be retained for as long as necessary to provide delivery services, comply with legal or contractual obligations, resolve disputes, and maintain security or audit logs.

## Security

RxEx Driver uses secure local storage for authentication tokens and transmits data to backend services over network connections controlled by RxExpresss infrastructure. No method of transmission or storage is completely risk-free, but reasonable safeguards are used to protect operational and personal information.

## Your Choices

- You may choose not to grant camera or location permissions, but parts of the app may not work correctly without them.
- Drivers can sign out of the app, which clears the stored authentication token from the device.

## Children

RxEx Driver is a workforce application for authorized delivery personnel and is not directed to children.
