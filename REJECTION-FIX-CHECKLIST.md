# Apple Rejection Fix Checklist

Use this checklist before resubmitting RxEx Driver to App Review.

- Delete all Android screenshots from App Store Connect.
- Upload only real iPhone screenshots captured from TestFlight or the iOS Simulator.
- Do not upload iPad screenshots while iPad support is disabled.
- Do not use Android screenshots, Android status bars, VoLTE icons, Android battery icons, or Android navigation bars.
- Screenshots must reflect iPhone iOS UI only.
- Confirm App Review notes explain that accounts are created internally by RxExpresss administrators and pharmacy partners.
- Confirm the reviewer account has Driver access and at least one demo delivery assigned.
- Confirm the camera pre-permission screen uses a neutral Continue button before the native permission prompt.
- Confirm the denied camera state shows Open Settings only after permission has already been denied.
