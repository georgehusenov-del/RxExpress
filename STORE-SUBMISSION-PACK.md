## Product Summary

App name: RxEx Driver

Core purpose:
RxEx Driver is a delivery operations app for pharmacy drivers. It lets drivers sign in securely, review assigned stops, scan packages, capture proof of delivery, collect copays, log failed attempts, and send foreground route updates while on duty.

## Google Play Listing

### Short description
Secure pharmacy delivery app for assigned stops, package scans, and proof of delivery.

### Full description
RxEx Driver helps delivery drivers complete pharmacy deliveries with speed, accuracy, and accountability.

With RxEx Driver, authorized drivers can:
- Sign in securely and restore their work session
- View assigned deliveries and delivery history
- Scan package QR codes before delivery
- Update delivery progress in real time
- Capture proof of delivery with photos, signatures, notes, and coordinates
- Record copay collection and failed attempts
- Share live foreground location updates while on duty

RxEx Driver is intended for authorized delivery personnel working with RxExpresss operations. It is not a consumer shopping app.
